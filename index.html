FLIGHT MANUAL / 001
LESS THINKING.
MORE FLYING.

    
01 / STEER
Move your palm a little left, right, up or down. Or use WASD / arrow keys. On touchscreens, drag the world.
02 / SHOOT
Pinch thumb + index and hold. Or hold Space / click / FIRE. Your targets are THE LATTICE: fast SEEKERS (2 hits) and armoured PYLONS (3 hits). Watch weapon heat.
03 / FLOW
Fly through cyan gates to build your chain. Hold Shift / BOOST for speed. In two-hand mode, pinch the steering hand to boost.

    
Two hands: screen-left pilots, screen-right aims and fires. Roles lock during setup. Press R to recenter. If tracking disappears, firing stops and the game pauses. Support your elbows and take breaks.
